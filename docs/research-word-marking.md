# Research: marking the word being learned

Status: built and accepted on the device on 2026-10-11, released in `v2.3.0`.

## Why

The user mines vocabulary, not sentences. Before the app, an unknown word meant a screenshot, and
later a pass through the gallery: open each screenshot, read it, type the word into a spreadsheet,
look it up, delete the screenshot. The app replaced the screenshot with a captured line, but still
left finding the word to a later reread. Marking the word in the list removes that step and gives
the two columns the spreadsheet needs from the app: the word and its example sentence.

The spreadsheet has one row per word: Word, Phonetic, Definition, Example (the word in bold), Anki
Back (generated by a script) and Added. Ten cards a day go into Anki, Example on the front.

## Decided

- A tap on a word marks it, a second tap unmarks it. Several words can be marked in one line.
- Marks are stored as word positions in the line (`markedWordIndices`), not as text.
- A word is a run of letters or digits, with an apostrophe or hyphen allowed inside it. Punctuation
  around it and the " / " between joined lines are not part of any word.
- A marked word is drawn in the accent color with a faint tint behind it. Never in bold: a heavier
  weight widens the word and shifts the rest of the line, which the user rejected on the device.
- A tap on a line no longer copies it. Copying goes through the long-press selection.
- Each capture keeps its date, and the list is grouped by day with a count, to follow the ten a day.
- A line already in the list is never added again.
- Deleting is rare, so it has no button on the card. It lives in the selection bar.

## Rejected

- **Dragging across words to mark a run.** It is the same gesture as scrolling the list. Taps cover
  the rare three-word case.
- **A checkbox on the card during selection.** Anything added to the card moves the text. Selection
  only recolors the card.
- **Swapping a capture for the previous or next line**, for badly timed subtitle tracks. It cannot
  be automatic, because the app does not see the screen. It would need the two neighbouring lines
  stored with each capture and a gesture in the list. Left out until the need shows up in use.
- **Storing the episode and the position** with each capture. They remove no step from the user's
  workflow.

## Open

- An expression with something in the middle ("write it off") is marked as two separate words.
  Whether that becomes one spreadsheet row ("write off") or two is decided when the export is
  built. The proposal: one line gives one row, with its marked words joined in order.
- Sending the captures to the spreadsheet. The user's idea is that a capture leaves the list once
  it is sent, which would also retire the copy action.
